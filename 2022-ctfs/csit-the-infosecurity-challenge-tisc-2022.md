# CSIT The InfoSecurity Challenge (TISC) 2022

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (800).png>)

#### **Theme for TISC 2022**

PALINDROME is back again with a vengeance. Singapore is facing yet another large-scale cyber attack that compromised and disrupted several of Singapore's critical infrastructure and cyberspace.

There will be a series of challenges for Cybersecurity experts to complete in order to put a halt to PALINDROME's operations. The levels will cover topics from Web Pen-testing, Forensics, Reverse Engineering, Binary Exploitation, Cryptography, Mobile Security and Cloud.
