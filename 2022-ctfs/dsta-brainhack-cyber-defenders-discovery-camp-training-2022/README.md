# DSTA BrainHack Cyber Defenders Discovery Camp Training 2022

I participated with my initials `RZ` and finished `14/665` for this training.&#x20;

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (535).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (527).png>)

I felt that I learned a lot through this training and it broadened my knowledge in cybersecurity. This year's training was slightly different from last year [CDDC Training 2021](https://gadiel-lau.gitbook.io/2021-writeups/dsta-brainhack-cyber-defenders-discovery-camp-training-2021). I enjoyed this year's training more as I was able to apply my cybersecurity knowledge to solve challenges.

For the training, we had lecture videos covering topics such as - OSINT, Network Security, Web Exploitation, Cyber Forensics, Cryptography, Reverse Engineering and Binary Exploitation. I will not be posting the lecture contents here because its a few hundred pages and there might be copyright issues.

Besides the lecture videos, participants could solve up to 85 challenges in `Basic Concepts of Cybersecurity` and `CTF Topics`.&#x20;

I solved 60/85 challenges, of which 18/26 are in `Basic Concepts of Cybersecurity and` 42/59 in `CTF Topics`.&#x20;

![Basic Concepts of Cybersecurity](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (574).png>)

![CTF Topics](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (514).png>)

I thought the challenges were pretty interesting, especially `CTF Topics`, and I felt that I learned more through this "hands-on" training compared to just absorbing theory knowledge.

Hence, I decided to include these challenges in my writeups. I have split these challenges into its respective content category.
