I participated solo in this competition as `T34M1`through CTFtime platform. The event details can be found [here](https://ctftime.org/event/1149).

I only solved 1 challenge for this competition, partly due to the lack of time and the challenges being too tough for my level of experience.

I lost the challenge description and the screenshots of how I solved the challenge. I will give a brief writeup on how I solved the challenge.

On the VULNCON CTF platform, if I navigate to the instructions page, I will get the first part of the flag.

Next, to find the second part of the flag, we go to the rules page and we will see
