# SIT N0H4TS Cyber League 2022

The Cyber League is fronted by N0H4TS and aims to provide a competitive platform for all students and professionals alike to hone their cybersecurity skillsets while competing at the highest level. The Cyber League aims to discover and develop the future generations of cybersecurity talents through our multi-layered divisional leagues.

This season's difficulty is catered towards participants over the age of 21 (roughly around university level).

The Cyber League is the Next Generation Capture The Flag competition that will be held annually. The Cyber League consists of 3 Majors:

[MAJOR 1](https://cyberleague.co) - 30 Apr - 1 May 2022\
[COMMUNITY MAJOR(SEETF)](https://seetf.sg/seetf/) - 4 June - 6 June 2022\
[MAJOR 2](https://cyberleague.co) - 24 Sep - 25 Sep 2022
