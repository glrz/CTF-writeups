# PicoCTF 2023

This was my first time participating in PicoCTF competition. I participated as solo even though it allowed up to 5 members in a team.

I spent the first few days attempting the challenges and was pretty satisfied with the number of challenges I solved. I solved a total of 24 challenges in different categories such as Binary Exploitation, Cryptography, Forensics, General Skills, Reverse Engineering and Web Exploitation.
