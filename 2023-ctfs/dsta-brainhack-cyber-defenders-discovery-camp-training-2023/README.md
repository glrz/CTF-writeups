Overall, I felt that this training was quite useful in helping me learn new concepts in cybersecurity and tackle new challenges. I managed to apply new skills to solve challenges in areas such us `OSINT`, `Network Security` and `Cyber Forensics` under `CTF Topics`. However, it could have been better in various aspects.&#x20;

First, the lectures covered should be more closely related to the challenges in `CTF Topics`. Additionally, I would have prefered the Discord ticket support staff to be more helpful in answering my queries. Finally, some of the challenge descriptions were vague and there were typos as well, which caused delay and misunderstanding in the submission of flags.

I will be doing writeups for all the challenges solved. If you are interested, you can check out previous year CDDC 2022 training which I participated [here](https://gadiel-lau.gitbook.io/2022-writeups/2022-ctfs/dsta-brainhack-cyber-defenders-discovery-camp-training-2022).
