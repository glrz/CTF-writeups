# AYCEP 2023

The Advanced Youth Cyber Exploration Programme (AYCEP) is an online cybersecurity bootcamp under CSA's SG Cyber Youth Odyssey framework. The programme is intended for students who have participated in other cybersecurity bootcamps or Capture-The-Flag competitions to continue their cybersecurity journey, to deepen their skills and continue their cybersecurity journey.

This year's AYCEP training bootcamp will take place on the 6th of June 2023. The top 60 participants of the bootcamp will be invited to compete in the CTF finale physically at the ASEAN – Singapore Cybersecurity Centre for Excellence (ASCCE) on 7th June 2023.
