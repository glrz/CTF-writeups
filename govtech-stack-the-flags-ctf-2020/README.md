# GovTech STACK the Flags CTF 2020



I participated in GovTech STACK the Flags CTF 2020 with team name: ScriptKiddiex as the team leader, with 3 other teammates – Ho Xin Ying, Choa Wee Jun and Sonny. I solved all the challenges here except one which was solved by my teammate Xin Ying. I am satisfied with the results as we are in the top 50 out of 245 teams.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (251).png>)

The challenges were split up into different categories – Introduction, Binary Exploitation, Cloud, Cryptography, Forensics, Internet of Things, Open Source Intelligence, Reverse Engineering, Web, Miscellaneous and Mobile.
